## 摘要
在超五代（B5G）和第六代（6G）网络中，工业服务越来越多地以多阶段工作流的形式执行，其服务质量（QoS）需求在有序阶段之间不断变化。现有的暴露、分析和策略控制功能可以提供能力信息并实施QoS处理，但它们不跟踪工作流与网络之间已接受的QoS协议。本文研究了软件控制平面中这一缺失的协调功能。我们提出了一种能力代理，将每个已接受的轨迹表示为生命周期管理的QoS承诺，包括工作流需求、网络能力、有效时间和执行状态。该代理在现有的暴露和策略控制接口之上运行，并强制实施能力新鲜度、重复安全的准入、合法的状态转换、每承诺事件排序、恢复路由以及代理-网络状态一致性。通过微基准测试、故障注入、消融实验和受控消息丢失进行的原型结果表明，完整的代理以微秒级的本地处理开销保持承诺一致性，而移除单个代理功能则会暴露重复承诺、陈旧准入、非法转换、丢失更新分歧或代理-网络不一致等问题。

## Abstract
Industrial services in beyond-fifth-generation (B5G) and sixth-generation (6G) networks are increasingly executed as multi-phase workflows whose quality-of-service (QoS) demands change across ordered phases. Existing exposure, analytics, and policy-control functions can provide capability information and enforce QoS treatment, but they do not track the admitted QoS agreement between a workflow and the network. This paper studies this missing coordination function in the software control plane. We propose a Capability Broker that represents each admitted trajectory as a lifecycle-managed QoS commitment, including the workflow demand, network capability, validity time, and enforcement state. The Broker operates above existing exposure and policy-control interfaces and enforces capability freshness, duplicate-safe admission, legal state transitions, per-commitment event ordering, recovery routing, and Broker--network state consistency. Prototype results with microbenchmarks, fault injection, ablation, and controlled message loss show that the full Broker preserves commitment consistency with microsecond-level local processing overhead, while removing individual Broker functions exposes duplicate commitments, stale admissions, illegal transitions, lost-update divergences, or Broker-network inconsistency.
