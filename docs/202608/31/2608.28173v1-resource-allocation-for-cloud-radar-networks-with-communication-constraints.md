## 摘要
分布式雷达感知利用空间分集来解决遮挡问题并提高估计精度。然而，实现这些增益依赖于将高维雷达数据传输到融合中心（FC）。这对无线网络提出了重大要求，尤其是在工厂等密集、动态且易受干扰的环境中，在这些环境中，弹性和延迟至关重要。本文研究了容量受限的两跳云雷达网络中的资源分配问题。我们提出了一种缓冲接入协议，其中传感器执行局部频谱加窗以降低数据速率，然后通过中间边缘服务器（ES）将测量结果传输到FC。资源分配被表述为一个混合整数优化问题，旨在在满足前传和回传容量约束的前提下，最小化目标参数的聚合克拉美-罗下界（CRLB）。我们开发了一种基于Big-M公式和连续凸近似（SCA）的迭代求解算法。所提出的框架有效地识别了信息量最大的传感器子集，并优化了时频资源分配，确保在严格的通信预算内实现高保真感知。

## Abstract
Distributed radar sensing exploits spatial diversity to resolve occlusions and improve estimation accuracy. Realizing these gains, however, relies on the transmission of high-dimensional radar data to a Fusion Center (FC). This imposes significant demands on the wireless network, especially in dense, dynamic, and interference-prone environments like factories, where resilience and latency are critical. This paper studies the resource allocation problem in a capacity-constrained two-hop cloud radar network. We propose a buffered access protocol where sensors perform local spectral windowing to reduce data rates before transmitting measurements to the FC via intermediate Edge Servers (ESs). The resource allocation is formulated as a mixed-integer optimization problem aimed at minimizing the aggregate Cramer-Rao Lower Bound (CRLB) of the target parameters subject to fronthaul and backhaul capacity constraints. We develop an iterative solution algorithm based on Big-M formulation and Successive Convex Approximation (SCA). The proposed framework efficiently identifies the most informative sensor subsets and optimizes time-frequency resource assignments, ensuring high-fidelity sensing within stringent communication budgets.
